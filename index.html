Internship · StartupWith my professor at SRMIn progress

Speedy Fix

A car service platform for a startup. Your car gets picked up, serviced and brought back, and you can see every step on the way. One landing page, four portals: customers, pickup drivers, service centres and admins.

See it live ↗
Read the whole story
  1. The spark

    A senior brought me onto a real startup as an intern, working alongside my professor at SRM.

  2. The problem

    Getting a car serviced costs you a day, and you have no idea what’s happening to it. You just have to trust the garage.

  3. The rabbit hole

    Designing for four kinds of people at once, each needing something different from the same car.

  4. The making

    A scroll-driven car journey that tells the whole service story on the landing page, then a separate portal for every role.

  5. The creation

    A working frontend for all four portals, live on Vercel, ready for its backend.

  6. The afterthought

    The hardest part wasn’t the code. It was seeing the real problem: four kinds of people, one car. That was also the most fun part.

HEARD · ROUTED · RESOLVED
Product · Web + AndroidDalmia Cement, Rajgangpur plant

Grievance Portal

A portal where plant employees raise and track workplace grievances, from housing to safety, with AI that suggests how urgent each one is and an HR dashboard for the whole plant.

See it live ↗
Read the whole story
  1. The spark

    In a plant of thousands, a complaint can pass through many hands and still go nowhere.

  2. The problem

    Make it simple and safe for any employee to be heard, and make it impossible for a grievance to get lost.

  3. The rabbit hole

    25 categories, 13 departments, confidential submissions, and learning how a cement plant actually runs.

  4. The making

    AI suggests the priority and explains why. Routing to a department follows clear rules and is never guessed.

  5. The creation

    A web app and an Android app with status tracking, comments, notifications, ratings and HR analytics.

  6. The afterthought

    Let AI help, but don’t let AI decide. People trust a system they can understand.

A giftFor Papa’s 54th birthday

Papa’s Record Room

A room where Papa’s favourite songs play while our family photos turn on a turntable, with a shelf of his books that opens each one where he stopped reading.

Read the whole story
  1. The spark

    Papa always buys his own gifts, so I couldn’t surprise him with a thing. I had to make one.

  2. The problem

    Make a gift he would use every day, not one that sits on a shelf.

  3. The rabbit hole

    319 of his Hindi favourites, 100 English ones, years of family photos and his whole reading list.

  4. The making

    The turntable plays only his songs. Photos spin like records. Every book reopens where he left off.

  5. The creation

    And “Tum Se Hi Ghar”, a birthday song written from Mumma’s, Bhaiya’s and my words. Leo & Shiro contributed too.

  6. The afterthought

    The best brief I’ve ever had came from knowing the person really well.

Still going · not giving up

The year-long app

SafeFund, for my client Aditya. His workers collected payments from his clients offline, with cash, paper and confusion about who paid what. I took it on almost a year ago. Building it was the fun part. Then came the Play Store, and finding time for it alongside everything else.

Learned: shipping is its own skill, and it’s harder than building. Coming back to something for a year isn’t failing. It’s commitment.

Ongoing · embarrassing

24 repos, very few READMEs

I start fast and explain later. Later rarely comes. Some of my repositories are just a title and a hope.

Learned: future me is also a user. She deserves notes too.

Recurring · every few weeks

Finishing one hobby

Every new obsession arrives with new stationery. Very few of them leave.

Learned: nothing is wasted. Every hobby left me a skill I used somewhere else.
